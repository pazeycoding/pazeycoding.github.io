Website FAQ

Do you use templates?

My websites are custom designed and developed around each business. I don't take your business and simply drop it into a generic layout.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Absolutely. A project can start from scratch or build on an existing website when an update, redesign or technical improvement makes more sense.

Do I have to pay monthly fees to keep my website active?

No monthly fees necessary! The only fee you will see after the website build is the domain (website name) cost which is only $25-$30 per YEAR

Can I request changes before my website launches?

Absolutely! Your package includes a review period for the website and any requests or adjustments can be made before launch.

Can I update my website after it launches?

Yes! You can request updates through Pazey at the applicable hourly rate, or choose the Website Care & Growth plan for ongoing support and up to two hours of website edits per month.

Do I own my website?

Yes! You own the code to your website that you paid for and can request it at any time.
